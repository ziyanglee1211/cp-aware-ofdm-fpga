# Provenance and preparation record

Original scientific files were copied without changing their bytes. `provenance/original_file_hashes.json` identifies release-relative locations and source digests; the private absolute-source copy manifest is retained locally and is not part of the public payload.

The manuscript reference PDFs are linked to the author-locked 14 October submission snapshot. The archived formal protocol, raw numerical records and all selected continuation finals preserve their own original hashes. This package introduces new packaging/checking utilities and documentation, not a new performance experiment or a rewritten research protocol.

OpenAI Codex assisted with original research code, plotting support and manuscript revision as disclosed in the current manuscript, and with this release preparation. The scientific-writing workflow required explicit source binding, a separation of packaging checks from scientific replication, and the exclusion of private author/submission material. Automated checks do not replace accountable authors' review or publication approval.
